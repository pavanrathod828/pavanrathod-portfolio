## 2. Target Audiences

This portfolio serves three audiences simultaneously. Every page, section, and copy choice should consciously serve at least one of them. If a section serves none, cut it.

- **Who:** Recruiters at Google, Meta, Microsoft, Amazon, Apple, Nvidia, Anthropic, OpenAI screening for SWE intern / new-grad / early-career roles.

## 7. Anti-patterns — Things this portfolio will NOT do

*This section is more important than any positive guidance. The default failure mode of an AI-assisted portfolio is to do too much. List things you commit to NOT doing so future-you can't talk yourself into them.*

- ❌ No "Hi, I'm Pavan 👋" hero. Wave emoji is the universal signal of generic AI portfolio.
- ❌ No 3D blob / WebGL hero background. They're slow, dated, and read as "I learned Three.js this weekend."
- ❌ No skill bars / percentage proficiency widgets. They're unmeasurable and recruiters ignore them.
- ❌ No "currently learning" section. Either you know it well enough to ship with, or it doesn't go on the portfolio.
- ❌ No AI-generated hero illustration or avatar. Use a real photo or no photo.
- ❌ No animated typewriter effect on the headline.
- ❌ No infinite scroll. Footer must be reachable in one scroll.
- ❌ No "Download Resume" as the only CTA. Email + LinkedIn must be one click away from everywhere on the site.
- ❌ No projects without a live link OR a GitHub link. If both are private, the project doesn't go on the site.
- ❌ No more than 6 projects featured. Anything beyond goes on a `/archive` page or doesn't appear.
- ❌ No tutorial follow-along projects (e.g., "I built a Twitter clone following X course").
